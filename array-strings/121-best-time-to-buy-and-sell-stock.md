# Problem: Best time to buy and sell stock (Easy)
[Link](https://leetcode.com/problems/best-time-to-buy-and-sell-stock/)

## Approach
The solution goes through the given list of stock prices and keeps track of the
lowest and highest prices and uses that to find the the best time to purchase
and the generated profit.

## Complexity
- Time: $O(n)$
- Space: $O(1)$
